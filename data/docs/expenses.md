# Halden Robotics: Travel and Expenses Policy

Meals while travelling are covered up to £35 per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

Hotels are covered up to £160 per night in London and £110 per night elsewhere in the UK.

Book trains in standard class. First class is allowed only for journeys longer than 3 hours.

Submit receipts in the Spendly app within 30 days of the expense. Your line manager approves claims under £500; claims of £500 or more also need approval from finance.

Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll.
