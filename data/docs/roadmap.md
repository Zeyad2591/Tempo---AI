# Product Roadmap, Q4 2026 to Q1 2027 (Internal)

The Ardent X3 launches on 3 December 2026. Its internal codename is Nightjar. Headline changes from the X2: payload rises to 10 kg and reach to 1,100 mm.

Fleet API v2 ships in January 2027. It adds webhooks and job batching. Fleet API v1 is retired on 30 June 2027.

The Starter plan price stays the same in 2027. The Team plan rises to £169 per month from 1 April 2027 for new customers.

Do not share launch dates outside the company before the public announcement.
