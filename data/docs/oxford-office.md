# Oxford Office Guide

The Oxford office is at 14 Kestrel Yard, Oxford OX4 2JY.

The building is open from 07:30 to 20:00 on weekdays. Weekend access needs approval from facilities.

The staff Wi-Fi network is halden-staff. Visitors use halden-guest, and the guest password changes every Monday.

Bike storage is in the courtyard, with 40 spaces and 6 charging points for e-bikes.

The nearest bus stop is Cowley Centre, a 4-minute walk.
