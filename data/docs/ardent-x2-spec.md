# Ardent X2 Robot Arm: Technical Specification

The Ardent X2 is Halden's six-axis collaborative robot arm for light assembly and lab automation.

- Payload: 7.5 kg
- Reach: 950 mm
- Repeatability: ±0.03 mm
- Weight: 24 kg
- Power: 48 V DC, typical draw 180 W
- Operating temperature: 5 °C to 40 °C
- Ingress protection: IP54
- Maximum tool speed: 1.8 m/s

The X2 ships with a 3-year hardware warranty. Joint motors are user-replaceable; a spare joint module costs £1,250.
