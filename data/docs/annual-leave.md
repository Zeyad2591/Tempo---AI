# Halden Robotics: Annual Leave Policy

Every full-time employee gets 27 days of annual leave per year, plus UK bank holidays. Part-time employees get a pro rata amount.

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. You can carry over at most 5 unused days into the next year, and carried-over days must be used by 31 March.

Book leave in the Leavely app at least 14 days in advance for any absence longer than 2 days. Shorter absences need 3 days' notice. Your line manager approves requests.

The office closes between Christmas and New Year. Those 3 closure days come out of your 27 days.

After 5 years of service you get 2 extra days of leave per year.
