# Halden Robotics: Customer Refund Policy

Customers can request a full refund within 45 days of delivery for any Halden robot arm or accessory. After 45 days, refunds are only available for hardware faults covered by the warranty.

Returned hardware that is not faulty carries a restocking fee of 12% of the purchase price. Faulty hardware has no restocking fee.

Refund requests go to returns@haldenrobotics.example with the order number. The returns team replies within 2 working days and issues a prepaid shipping label.

Refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 10 working days of the returned item arriving at the Didcot warehouse.

Software subscriptions (Fleet Console) can be cancelled at any time. Monthly plans are not refunded for the current month; annual plans are refunded pro rata for unused full months.
