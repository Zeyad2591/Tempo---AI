# Fleet Console Pricing, 2026

Fleet Console is the software Halden customers use to monitor and program their robot arms.

| Plan | Price | Robots included |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | £29 per month | Up to 3 robots |
| Team | £149 per month | Up to 25 robots |
| Enterprise | Custom quote | Unlimited robots, SSO, audit log |

Paying annually gives an 18% discount on Starter and Team.

Each robot above the plan limit costs £6 per month on Starter and £5 per month on Team.

Education and non-profit customers get 40% off Team after verification.
