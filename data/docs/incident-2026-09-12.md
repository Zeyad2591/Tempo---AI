# Postmortem: Fleet Gateway Outage, 12 September 2026

Summary: customers could not reach Fleet Console or send jobs to their robots for 47 minutes, from 14:06 to 14:53 UK time.

Root cause: the TLS certificate on the fleet gateway expired. Automatic renewal had been switched off during a load-balancer migration in August and was never switched back on.

Impact: 312 customer sites lost remote control. Robots already running jobs kept running safely.

Fixes: certificate renewal is automated again, and a new alert fires 21 days before any certificate expires. Owner: Liam Doyle.
