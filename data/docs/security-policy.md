# Halden Robotics: Information Security Basics

Every account uses multi-factor authentication with a hardware security key. Two keys are issued to each employee; keep the spare at home.

Passwords do not expire on a schedule, but must be at least 16 characters and stored in the company password manager.

Report suspected phishing to security@haldenrobotics.example within 1 hour of noticing it. Do not forward the suspicious email to colleagues.

All laptops use full-disk encryption. A lost or stolen laptop must be reported to the IT desk the same day.

Customer robot telemetry is classified Confidential and may not be copied to personal devices.
