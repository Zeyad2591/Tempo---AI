# Fleet Console Enterprise Service Level Agreement

Enterprise customers get a monthly uptime commitment of 99.95% for Fleet Console and the fleet gateway.

Support response times:

- P1 (production robots stopped): first response within 30 minutes, 24/7
- P2 (degraded): within 4 hours, business days
- P3 (questions): within 1 business day

Service credits: for every 0.1 percentage point of monthly uptime below 99.95%, the customer receives a credit of 10% of that month's fee, up to a maximum of 50%.

Planned maintenance is announced 7 days ahead and does not count against uptime.
