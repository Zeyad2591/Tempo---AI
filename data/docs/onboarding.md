# New Starter Onboarding

On your first day, collect your laptop and security keys from the IT desk in room B214 at 09:30.

Company induction runs every Wednesday at 10:00 in the Kestrel meeting room. Every new starter attends the first induction after their start date.

Each new starter is paired with an onboarding buddy for their first 6 weeks. Your manager tells you who your buddy is before your first day.

Your probation period is 3 months, with a check-in at the end of month 1 and a review at the end of month 3.
